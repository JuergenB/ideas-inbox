# Pricing Deep-Dive — Is PressRanger Actually Cheaper?

**The question (Juergen, 2026-06-12):** *"Do a deep investigation of their pricing. It seems like perhaps the press release distribution is not that much less expensive than elsewhere… it also could be that it is a function of our subscription and the capabilities it has."*

**Short answer: your instinct is correct.** PressRanger's *press-release distribution* is **mid-market, not a steal**, and it is **not discounted by our subscription** — anyone with a free account pays the same per-release price. The thing that *is* a genuine bargain is the **software** (media database + CRM + AI + pitch radar), which we own outright as a lifetime deal and which costs **$3,000–$40,000/year** as a standalone product elsewhere. The two are billed separately and should be evaluated separately.

All figures retrieved 2026-06-12; URLs and quotes in [sources.md](sources.md).

---

## PressRanger bills two unrelated things

PressRanger's own pricing page is explicit: *"PR software is billed monthly. Press release distribution is billed per release. Use either (or both)."* And the FAQ settles your "function of our subscription" hypothesis directly:

> **"Do I need to be a paying subscriber to use Wholesale press release distribution? No. Everyone can publish press releases at wholesale prices… Just create a free account."**

So the $299/$399 distribution price is **identical for free and paid users**. Our lifetime subscription buys us **zero** distribution discount. The "wholesale / members-only pricing" language is marketing — "member" just means "anyone who registered."

## Part 1 — Distribution (per release): where PressRanger actually sits

PressRanger's distribution runs on a GlobeNewswire-style syndication network with **guaranteed placements** (Business Insider, Yahoo Finance, AP News, Apple News, Benzinga, Google News, etc.):

- **Premium — $299/release** (advertised as "60% off" a $699 "retail"): 400+ placements, max domain authority 94, 2 images + 1 video, AI-chatbot indexing "sometimes."
- **Gold — $399/release** (advertised "60% off" $1,099): 500+ placements, max DA 97, **AIWire** (guaranteed indexing into ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini/Grok/Copilot + AI Visibility Report), **podcast distribution**, **newsroom-terminal** submissions (Bloomberg, Dow Jones Factiva, Reuters).

Now place that against the market, cheapest → most premium (per release, guaranteed-placement wires):

| Service | Price/release | What you get | Credibility |
|---------|--------------|--------------|-------------|
| IssueWire | ~$21 | 300+ syndicated sites | Low-authority pickup |
| NewswireNEXT / PRLog / 24-7 | ~$49 | 100–300+ sites | Budget |
| PR Underground | ~$75 | SEO + social | Budget |
| **EIN Presswire** | **$149** (or **~$67–100/ea in annual bundles**: $499/5, $999/15) | AP News, Google News, Bing; global | **Best budget value; legit** |
| **PressRanger Premium** | **$299** | 400+ placements, DA≤94 | Mid |
| **PressRanger Gold** | **$399** | 500+ placements, DA≤97, **AIWire**, podcasts, terminals | Mid + AI-indexing edge |
| **eReleases** | **$399 / $499 / $699** | **Real PR Newswire national distribution**; **CauseWire nonprofit discount** | High (PR Newswire reach) |
| Business Wire | $760+ | Tier-1 wire | High |
| **PR Newswire** | **$805+ national** (+$275/100 extra words; +$195–249/yr; real invoices **$1,500–$3,000**) | Tier-1, brand-name | Highest |

### What this table says

1. **PressRanger distribution is NOT the cheapest.** EIN Presswire ($149, or ~$67–100/release in bundles) delivers comparable budget-wire syndication for roughly **half** PressRanger's price.
2. **At its own price point, PressRanger is out-valued on reach.** **eReleases starts at $399 — the same as PressRanger Gold — but distributes over the actual PR Newswire network and offers a nonprofit (CauseWire) discount.** For genuine credibility per dollar, eReleases wins at that price.
3. **PressRanger's one distinctive distribution feature is AIWire** — guaranteed indexing into AI chatbots (ChatGPT/Perplexity/etc.). That's a real, current differentiator if "show up when someone asks an AI about [our show]" matters. Nobody else in this table guarantees it.
4. **It IS cheaper than PR Newswire** ($299–399 vs. $1,500–3,000 real-world) — but so is everyone else. "Cheaper than the most expensive option" is a low bar.

**Verdict on distribution:** it's a **price-shoppable commodity**, not the reason to use PressRanger. Match the wire to the story:
- Routine open call → **skip the paid wire** (rely on targeted pitches + free aggregator listings + paid social), or EIN Presswire ($149) if cheap SEO syndication helps.
- Want AI-chatbot visibility for an announcement → **PressRanger Gold ($399)**.
- Marquee, credibility-critical story (flagship grant, major partnership) → **eReleases / CauseWire** (PR Newswire reach at nonprofit rates).

## Part 2 — Software (subscription): where the real value is

This is the half our lifetime deal covers. Standalone, this category is expensive:

| Tool | Annual cost (software only) | Notes |
|------|----------------------------|-------|
| **PressRanger PR software** | **Free / $79 / $149 per *month*** retail (we pay **$0** — lifetime deal) | DB + CRM + AI + pitch radar |
| Prowly | ~$3,096/yr ($258/mo) | Closest realistic paid alternative |
| Muck Rack | Opaque, annual; low-mid four figures+ | Better data quality |
| Cision | ~$10,000–$15,000/yr | Enterprise |
| Meltwater | ~$15,000–$40,000+/yr | Enterprise |

PressRanger's own retail software pricing is **$79/mo (Pro)** or **$149/mo (Pro+)** — i.e. **~$948–$1,788/year** if we were paying. We're not. **That** is the spend our AppSumo lifetime deal eliminates, and it's the only part of PressRanger that's genuinely a bargain for us.

## Bottom line for the decision

| If the goal is… | Use… | Because |
|-----------------|------|---------|
| A **media database + outreach CRM + AI drafting** | **PressRanger (owned, $0)** | Standalone equivalents are $3K–$40K/yr; we already have Tier 3 |
| **Cheap SEO/syndication** distribution | EIN Presswire (~$149, less in bundles) | ~½ PressRanger's price for similar budget reach |
| **AI-chatbot indexing** of an announcement | PressRanger Gold ($399) | Only one here that guarantees AIWire |
| **Credible, premium reach** (marquee story) | eReleases / CauseWire | Real PR Newswire network + nonprofit discount |
| ❌ Justify the tool on its **wire being cheap** | — | It isn't, and the price isn't tied to our subscription |

The headline for the README and the team: **we should keep and use PressRanger for its software, not its wire.** The wire is just another line item we shop per release. The reason PressRanger is worth our time is the owned database + CRM + AI + pitch radar — the part that would otherwise cost thousands a year.
