# Where to List Open Calls — Submission Platforms & Amplifier Media

The directory the Thursday meeting asked for: the sites where **artists actually go to find open calls** (so we list our calls there), plus the **journalists, newsletters, and podcasts** that can amplify a call. This is the second table of the Airtable contact repository (the first being individual media contacts from PressRanger).

Compiled 2026-06-12 from web research + a Perplexity session; URLs and retrieval notes in [sources.md](sources.md). **Free / paid status and exact fees change often — verify on each site's "Submit an Opportunity / For Organizations" page before relying on it.** Where a fee is uncertain it's marked *(confirm)*.

> Two categories below: **(A) listing/aggregator boards** where you post the call, and **(B) submission-management software** that both runs your jurying *and* lists the call. CaFÉ and Submittable are category B (we may already use one for intake); the category-A boards are mostly free amplification we're probably leaving on the table today.

## B. Submission-management software (runs jurying AND lists the call)

| Platform | URL | Cost (organizer) | Notes |
|----------|-----|------------------|-------|
| **CaFÉ (CallForEntry.org)** | callforentry.org | **Annual subscription + per-recipient region/discipline fee + processing fees**; tiers for nonprofits/independent galleries (<250 submissions) vs. agencies/universities (fixed, high-volume) | The **US standard** for juried shows & public-art RFQs; what most US arts nonprofits use. If we run intake here, the call is also discoverable to CaFÉ's artist base. |
| **Submittable** | submittable.com | **$399/mo (5 users) · $799/mo (20) · $1,499/mo (50)** | General-purpose; powerful but **expensive and overkill** for our volume. Better fit for large grant programs than routine open calls. |
| **EntryThingy** | entrythingy.com | Free tier to host (you set entry fees) | Doubles as listing board (above) — best free option for both jurying-lite and discovery. |

## C. Amplifier media — journalists, newsletters, podcasts

These don't host the call; they **broadcast** it. Pitch them the story (and feed their contacts into the PressRanger-sourced Airtable). Several run recurring "opportunities" roundups — getting into those is free earned reach.

| Outlet | Reach / angle | How to reach | Notes |
|--------|---------------|--------------|-------|
| **Hyperallergic** | Major contemporary/emerging-art audience | **tips@hyperallergic.com** for tips/announcements; see hyperallergic.com/how-to-pitch; "Open Call" tag | The single highest-value amplifier for our audience. |
| **Colossal (thisiscolossal.com)** | Large general art/design audience | Monthly **Opportunities Newsletter** (open calls, grants, residencies) — submit to be included | Recurring roundup = repeatable placement. |
| **e-flux — Announcements** | International, institutional art world | Paid **announcement** submission | Trusted source for serious open calls; paid but credible. |
| **Create! Magazine** | Emerging-artist focused (our benchmark for disciplined cadence) | Submit a call / partner | Juergen cited them in the meeting as the model for "much more disciplined way of keeping in touch." |
| **Arts to Hearts Project** | Emerging + women artists; newsletter + IG "Open Call for Artists" | Submit-a-call / partner form | Strong social amplification for emerging-artist calls. |
| **The Art Newspaper** | International art news; daily + weekly podcast | Press/tips | For marquee announcements. |
| **NY Art Competitions Blog** | Competition-seeking artists | Listing/feature | Niche but on-target. |

## How PressRanger plugs into this

PressRanger's databases are for the **C-column work** (finding *individual* journalists, regional outlets, and arts/culture podcasts to pitch) — filter by category *art / arts & culture / visual arts / lifestyle* + location, then hand-verify and load into Airtable. The **A/B columns are mostly outside PressRanger** — they're a curated list we build and maintain ourselves (the meeting's "shared, structured list of venues/contacts in Airtable"). Together: PressRanger feeds the *who-to-pitch* table; this directory feeds the *where-to-post* table.

## Recommended starting set (Phase 1)

Don't boil the ocean. Start by posting every open call to the **free, high-reach boards** and pitching the **top amplifiers**:

1. **EntryThingy** + **ArtCallEntry** + **Artwork Archive** + **ArtConnect** — free listings, broad artist reach.
2. **CaFÉ** — if/when we want juried intake with the credibility US artists expect (it likely fits our existing process).
3. **Hyperallergic** (tips@) + **Colossal opportunities** + **Create! Magazine** — the three amplifiers most aligned with our audience.

Everything else gets added to the Airtable board over time, with free/paid and contact confirmed as we go.
