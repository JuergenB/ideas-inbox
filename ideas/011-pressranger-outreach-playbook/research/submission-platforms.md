# Where to Register Open Calls — US-Wide Directory (Platforms, Partners & Media)

The demand-generation layer of the engine: a US-wide map of **where to register an open call / exhibition** so more artists discover and submit, plus the **organizations and media** that amplify a call. This becomes the *Listing-Site & Partner Registry* table in our owned promotion repository (the extended Artwork Archive Airtable) — the meeting's "shared, structured list of submission venues."

Compiled 2026-06-12 from web research + grounded Perplexity sessions (`sonar` / `sonar-reasoning-pro`); URLs in [sources.md](sources.md).

> ⚠️ **Costs are approximate and must be confirmed.** Many fees below are model/analyst estimates and sources conflict (e.g. NYFA "free for affiliated orgs" vs. "paid Classifieds"). Treat every price as *(confirm on the site)*. Free-to-post status is the most valuable and the most likely to change — verify before relying on it.

## B. National organizations, councils & partners (amplification + partnership)

These don't just list — they're **partners** for national visibility, and several syndicate opportunities to large professional audiences.

| Organization | URL | What they are | How to engage |
|--------------|-----|---------------|---------------|
| **Americans for the Arts** | americansforthearts.org | National arts nonprofit (advocacy, research, Public Art Network) | Share calls via newsletters / Public Art Network; org membership; pitch program staff. |
| **College Art Association (CAA)** | collegeart.org | Membership assoc. for artists, scholars, curators; deep university reach | Post to Careers/Opportunities portal (membership/paywall); ad/sponsorship packages. |
| **National Endowment for the Arts (NEA)** | arts.gov/grants/grants-for-arts-projects | Federal funder | *Not* a listing board — partner by **applying for project funding** to underwrite our own artist-opportunity program; NEA-funded projects get press visibility. |
| **Creative Capital** | creative-capital.org/artist-resources/artist-opportunities | National grantmaker + curated **artist-opportunities** resource list | Their open call is for their own award; engage at program/co-presentation level; opportunities resource list is worth being on. |
| **ArtsThread** | artsthread.com | Platform for art/design **students & grads**, competitions | Partner as competition sponsor/host; they market it across schools + newsletters. |
| **Artist Communities Alliance (ACA)** | artistcommunities.org/directory/open-calls | Service org for residencies; **Open Calls directory** | Member programs listed + featured in comms; submit to the directory. |
| **State / regional arts councils** | e.g. arts.ca.gov/opportunities, maaa.org (Mid-America Arts Alliance) | State & regional councils publish opportunity/deadline roundups | Submit to each relevant council's opportunities page; strong regional reach. |

## How this plugs into the owned engine

- **Section A** = the *Listing-Site Registry* table (post every call to the free boards by default; CaFÉ for credible juried intake).
- **Section B** = the *Partner Registry* — relationships and syndication channels to cultivate over time (and the NEA-funding angle for Phase 2).
- **Section C** = pitched per campaign; **PressRanger's database is for finding the individual contacts** at these outlets, then hand-verified into our repository.

## Recommended Phase-1 starting set

Don't boil the ocean. For the first live call (*Art of Resistance*):
1. **Post to the free boards:** EntryThingy, ArtCallEntry, Artwork Archive, ArtConnect, Fractured Atlas, The Art List. *(CaFÉ if we want juried intake.)*
2. **Submit to recurring roundups:** This Is Colossal Opportunities, Art Opportunities Monthly, Create! Magazine, Booooooom.
3. **Pitch the top amplifier:** Hyperallergic (tips@).
4. **One partner channel:** the relevant state/regional arts council + Artist Communities Alliance if residency-adjacent.

Everything else gets added to the registry over time, with free/paid and contact confirmed as we go.
