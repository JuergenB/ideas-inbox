# Where to Register Open Calls & Reach Funders — US-Wide Master Directory

The demand-generation layer of the engine: where to **register open calls/exhibitions**, **reach press/partners**, and **find funding** — prioritizing **free, self-service, URL-based** registration. This becomes the *Listing-Site & Partner Registry* in the Artwork Archive Airtable (the art-data hub the PolyWiz engine reads from).

## 📊 The master: `arts-master-resource.csv`

The authoritative version is **[research/arts-master-resource.csv](arts-master-resource.csv)** — **61 verified rows**, built by **Perplexity Computer** (2026-06-12) and **team-refreshed 2026-06-18** via the [live directory app](https://arterial-art-exhibition-promotion-resources.pplx.app/). Every row was browsed and verified (an "Action URL Live" column) and now also carries a **"How to submit"** field, with per-row cost, self-service level, audience, geography, source, and verified date. **Import this CSV directly into the Airtable registry.** The tables below are a readable cross-section; the CSV is the source of truth.

> Costs verified as of June 2026 (team-refreshed 2026-06-18); cells marked *confirm* still need a final check. This **supersedes** the earlier hand-estimated figures — e.g. CaFÉ is actually **$225 onboarding + $250–475/call + $120/yr**; EntryThingy **$3/submission**; NYFA **$95–315/listing**.

**Coverage by workstream (refreshed):** Fundraising/Grants/Donor **24** · Open-Call Listing **14** · Media/PR Amplifier **14** · Partner Org/Council **6** · Paid-Ads Channel **3**. The team **folded the old "Submission Software" category into Open-Call Listing** — those tools (EntryThingy, ArtCall, Artwork Archive, ArtConnect) are listed for their free *listing* path, not their pay-to-host jurying. Pure submission-host platforms (Zealous, CaFÉ, Submittable, ZAPPlication) were pruned from the canonical CSV but kept in Section B below for reference.

### ★ Quick start — free + instant self-service (post today, no email)
ArtDeadline (`artdeadline.com/adc-news/`, free if entry fee ≤$50) · College Art Association (`collegeart.org/.../opportunities/submit/`, free) · InLiquid (Airtable form, free) · GivingTuesday toolkit (free) · Mid-America Arts Alliance (free). Many more are **free with a quick account** (next tables).
