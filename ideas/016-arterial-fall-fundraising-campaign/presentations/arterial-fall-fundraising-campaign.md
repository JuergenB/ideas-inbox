### Where we are starting

# Most people have never heard of Arterial

<div style="display: flex; gap: 20px; margin-top: 12px;">
  <div class="card" style="flex: 1;">
    <div class="kicker">What people know</div>
    <div style="font-size: 0.82em; color: var(--body); line-height: 1.7;">They know <strong>Not Real Art</strong>. Or they know <strong>Artsville USA</strong>. They follow one of them, they like the work, and that is where it ends.</div>
  </div>
  <div class="card" style="flex: 1;">
    <div class="kicker">What they do not know</div>
    <div style="font-size: 0.82em; color: var(--body); line-height: 1.7;"><strong>Arterial is the non-profit behind them.</strong> It is the registered 501(c)(3) that holds Not Real Art, Artsville USA, Classic Black and the rest.</div>
  </div>
</div>

<div style="margin-top: 20px; font-size: 0.82em; color: var(--t); line-height: 1.6;">You cannot ask someone to support an organisation they have not met. So the campaign's first job is not fundraising. It is <strong>introducing the family</strong>.</div>

### The tool, in one slide

# What we are using to do it

<div style="font-size: 0.86em; color: var(--body); line-height: 1.75; max-width: 900px; margin-top: 8px;">
We have built a piece of software called <strong>PolyWiz</strong>. You give it one thing to work from, either a web page or a PDF, and it writes <strong>months of social media posts</strong> from it and schedules them out over time.
</div>

<div style="display: flex; gap: 16px; margin-top: 22px;">
  <div class="card" style="flex: 1;">
    <div class="kicker">It writes in the brand's voice</div>
    <div style="font-size: 0.78em; color: var(--body); line-height: 1.65;">Each organisation has its own voice on file, so the posts sound like Arterial rather than like software.</div>
  </div>
  <div class="card" style="flex: 1;">
    <div class="kicker">It posts everywhere at once</div>
    <div style="font-size: 0.78em; color: var(--body); line-height: 1.65;">Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, Pinterest and more, each written the way that platform expects.</div>
  </div>
  <div class="card" style="flex: 1;">
    <div class="kicker">A person approves everything</div>
    <div style="font-size: 0.78em; color: var(--body); line-height: 1.65;">Nothing publishes on its own. Every post is reviewed and edited before it goes anywhere.</div>
  </div>
</div>

<div style="margin-top: 18px; font-size: 0.75em; color: var(--m); line-height: 1.6;">The point is not volume for its own sake. It is that a small team can keep up a steady, consistent presence for three months without it becoming somebody's second job.</div>

### The approach

# Introduce, amplify, then invite

<div style="display: flex; gap: 16px; margin-top: 16px; align-items: stretch;">
  <div class="card" style="flex: 1;">
    <div class="kicker" style="color: var(--blue);">First</div>
    <div style="font-size: 0.95em; color: var(--t); font-family: 'Outfit'; margin-bottom: 8px;">Introduce</div>
    <div style="font-size: 0.76em; color: var(--body); line-height: 1.65;">Who Arterial is, what it holds, the people and the work behind it. No mention of money at all.</div>
  </div>
  <div class="card" style="flex: 1;">
    <div class="kicker" style="color: var(--y);">Then</div>
    <div style="font-size: 0.95em; color: var(--t); font-family: 'Outfit'; margin-bottom: 8px;">Amplify</div>
    <div style="font-size: 0.76em; color: var(--body); line-height: 1.65;">Ask people to share it and tell someone who would care. Spreading the word counts as real support, not as a consolation prize.</div>
  </div>
  <div class="card" style="flex: 1;">
    <div class="kicker" style="color: var(--g);">Then</div>
    <div style="font-size: 0.95em; color: var(--t); font-family: 'Outfit'; margin-bottom: 8px;">Invite</div>
    <div style="font-size: 0.76em; color: var(--body); line-height: 1.65;">Ask for support directly and warmly, always tied to what a gift actually pays for.</div>
  </div>
</div>

<div style="margin-top: 20px; font-size: 0.8em; color: var(--t); line-height: 1.6;">What we are deliberately <strong>not</strong> doing: countdowns, last chances, "we are falling short", or any of the pressure tactics that make people feel handled.</div>

### The shape of it

# Two campaigns, three months

<div style="display: flex; gap: 20px; margin-top: 16px; align-items: stretch;">
  <div class="card" style="flex: 1;">
    <div class="kicker" style="color: var(--blue);">Month 1</div>
    <div style="font-size: 1.05em; color: var(--t); font-family: 'Outfit'; margin-bottom: 10px;">Meet Arterial</div>
    <div style="font-size: 0.78em; color: var(--body); line-height: 1.7;">A month of posts that only introduce. Who Arterial is, what sits under it, the people, the programmes, the work that already exists.<br><br>Posted often, so the picture builds up.<br><br><strong>Nothing is asked for.</strong></div>
  </div>
  <div class="card" style="flex: 1.15;">
    <div class="kicker" style="color: var(--g);">Months 2 and 3</div>
    <div style="font-size: 1.05em; color: var(--t); font-family: 'Outfit'; margin-bottom: 10px;">The fall campaign</div>
    <div style="font-size: 0.78em; color: var(--body); line-height: 1.7;">Now the campaign itself. Why this work matters, help us spread the word, and a warm, direct invitation to give.<br><br>By this point the audience knows who is asking, which is the whole reason for month one.<br><br><strong>The ask has earned its context.</strong></div>
  </div>
</div>

<div style="margin-top: 18px; font-size: 0.75em; color: var(--m); line-height: 1.6;">Month one keeps working after it ends. Those posts do not expire, and they are still introducing Arterial to new people while the campaign runs.</div>

### The register

# What a warm ask sounds like

<div style="display: flex; gap: 20px; margin-top: 14px; align-items: stretch;">
  <div class="card" style="flex: 1;">
    <div class="kicker" style="color: var(--g);">Yes</div>
    <div style="font-size: 0.78em; color: var(--body); line-height: 1.9;">"Arterial is making a documentary about an art movement nobody has put on screen. You can help fund it."<br><br>"The campaign runs through December 15."<br><br>"Worth backing if you care about this."</div>
  </div>
  <div class="card" style="flex: 1;">
    <div class="kicker" style="color: var(--r);">No</div>
    <div style="font-size: 0.78em; color: var(--body); line-height: 1.9;">"Only 6 days left."<br><br>"We are falling short of our goal."<br><br>"Without you, this ends."<br><br>"Last chance to make a difference."</div>
  </div>
</div>

<div style="margin-top: 18px; font-size: 0.82em; color: var(--t); line-height: 1.6;">Direct is fine. <strong>Pressure is not.</strong> This is the line we would most like your read on, because you have watched far more of these run than we have.</div>

### The growth idea

# Collaborator posts put us in front of new people

<div style="font-size: 0.84em; color: var(--body); line-height: 1.75; max-width: 900px; margin-top: 6px;">
On Instagram, two accounts can <strong>share a single post</strong>. It appears on both feeds, and both audiences see it. One post, two audiences, no advertising spend.
</div>

<div style="display: flex; gap: 16px; margin-top: 20px;">
  <div class="card" style="flex: 1;">
    <div class="kicker">The easy half</div>
    <div style="font-size: 0.78em; color: var(--body); line-height: 1.65;">Arterial's own family. Not Real Art and Artsville USA already have followings, and a campaign post can go out on those feeds too.</div>
  </div>
  <div class="card" style="flex: 1.3;">
    <div class="kicker" style="color: var(--a);">The bigger prize</div>
    <div style="font-size: 0.78em; color: var(--body); line-height: 1.65;"><strong>Collaborators from outside the family.</strong> Artists, arts organisations, aligned non-profits. Each one puts the campaign in front of an audience that has never heard of Arterial, and each one arrives as a recommendation rather than an advert.</div>
  </div>
</div>

<div style="margin-top: 20px; font-size: 0.84em; color: var(--t); line-height: 1.6;">This is <strong>your question far more than ours</strong>. Who should those partners be? That is a fundraising and relationships question, and it is where outside judgement is worth more than any software.</div>

### Where we would love your input

# Four things we are unsure about

<div style="font-size: 0.82em; color: var(--body); line-height: 2.0; margin-top: 10px; max-width: 940px;">
<strong>1.</strong> Is a month of pure introduction before any ask the right balance, or too long a wait?<br>
<strong>2.</strong> Is our line on warm-but-never-pressured right, or are we leaving money on the table?<br>
<strong>3.</strong> Which outside partners should we be approaching for shared posts?<br>
<strong>4.</strong> What do you normally see work in a fall campaign that we have not mentioned at all?
</div>
