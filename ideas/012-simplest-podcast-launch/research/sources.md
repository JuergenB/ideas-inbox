## 1. Riverside records remote guests via a browser link — no account/download, local HD tracks, up to 10

- **Sources:**
  - Riverside.fm Help Center / FAQ — https://riverside.com/faq
  - Content Monsta, "Podcast Guest Connection Guide – Riverside.fm" — https://contentmonsta.com/podcast-guest-connection-guide-riverside/
  - Riverside blog, "Remote Video: How to Record, Edit, Live Stream" — https://riverside.com/blog/remote-video
- **Retrieved:** July 2026
- **Key facts:** Guests click a link and join in the browser (no account, no install). Each
  participant is recorded on their own device in high quality ("local recording"), so a weak
  connection doesn't degrade the audio. Up to 10 participants (host + 9).
- **Used in deck:** slides 5, 6.

  - Riverside — homepage feature list — https://riverside.com/

## 5. Riverside paid plan ≈ $29/mo (≈ $24/mo annual) — the tier that unlocks hosting + AI + longer recording

- **Source:** Riverside — Plans & Pricing — https://riverside.com/pricing
- **Retrieved:** July 2026
- **Key facts:** Free $0 (2 hrs one-off, watermark, no hosting); **Pro ~$29/mo ($24/mo billed
  annually)** — hosting + AI tools + longer monthly recording; Grow ~$39/mo; Webinar ~$99/mo;
  Business custom.
- **⚠ verify before quoting — HIGH churn:** Some third-party listicles still show an older
  "$15/$19" structure. Trust the live pricing page and re-check the day the deck is finalized or
  shared. The deck uses "~$29/month" and an explicit "prices can change" caveat for this reason.
- **Used in deck:** slides 11, 13.

## 6. Spotify for Creators = free hosting + distribution; one-time Apple Podcasts submission; embeddable player

- **Sources:**
  - Spotify for Creators — Podcast features — https://creators.spotify.com/features/podcast
  - Spotify support — "Submitting your show to Apple Podcasts" — https://support.spotify.com/us/creators/article/submitting-your-show-to-apple-podcasts/
  - Spotify support — "Distributing your show to other platforms" — https://support.spotify.com/us/creators/article/distributing-your-show-to-other-platforms/
- **Retrieved:** July 2026
- **Key facts:** Free, unlimited hosting + distribution (formerly Spotify for Podcasters /
  Anchor). Apple Podcasts listing is a one-time RSS submission via Apple Podcasts Connect (free),
  then paste the Apple URL back into Spotify for Creators. Provides an embeddable player for a
  website.
- **Used in deck:** slides 4, 8, 9, 11.
