# Sources & References — Idea 010: Guideless Pricing & Tier Strategy

Audit trail for every external pricing claim and positioning statement made in the deck
(`presentations/guideless-pricing-strategy.md`). Pricing figures are approximate and were
current as of **June 2026** — re-check each vendor's page before quoting a number to anyone.

Underlying research: a Perplexity session (June 2026) comparing the screen-tutorial /
guide-generation tool category, plus each vendor's own pricing page. The email thread that
prompted this work is preserved in [`../docs/email-to-rokas.md`](../docs/email-to-rokas.md).

---

## Guideless — Free (up to 3 guides) and Pro ~$29/user/mo annual ($39 monthly)

- **Source:** Guideless pricing page; G2 listing
- **Date:** 2026
- **URLs:** https://guideless.ai/pricing/ · https://www.g2.com/products/guideless-guideless/pricing
- **Retrieved:** 2026-06 via Perplexity session
- **Claim:** Free tier up to 3 guides with AI narration and sharing; single Pro plan at ~$29/user/mo billed annually ($39 monthly) for unlimited guides, AI narration, analytics, MP4 export, no watermark.
- **Used in deck:** slides 2, 4, 7 ("Today there's nothing between $0 and $29")

## Guideless positions itself as "10× simpler" than Guidde and compares to Loom

- **Source:** Guideless own compare pages
- **Date:** 2026
- **URLs:** https://guideless.ai/compare/guidde-alternative/ · https://guideless.ai/compare/loom-alternative/
- **Retrieved:** 2026-06
- **Claim:** Guideless explicitly frames itself as the same AI-video concept "10× simpler," leaning into clarity, speed, and default AI voiceover, versus Guidde's heavier enterprise editing. It also contrasts itself with Loom ("structured, reusable software tutorials" vs "quick, human screen recordings").
- **Used in deck:** slides 3, 5 (positioning + the "control vs speed" principle)

## Guidde — AI-narration business tier roughly ~$50/user/mo

- **Source:** Guideless compare page; Slashdot comparison
- **Date:** 2026
- **URLs:** https://guideless.ai/compare/guidde-alternative/ · https://slashdot.org/software/comparison/Guidde-vs-Guideless/
- **Retrieved:** 2026-06
- **Claim:** Guidde uses tiered, higher-priced plans with AI narration and advanced features reserved for upper tiers (~$50/user/mo for the AI-narration business tier).
- **Used in deck:** slides 3, 4

## Loom — Business / AI plans in the mid-teens per user/mo, plus a free Starter

- **Source:** Atlassian / Loom pricing
- **Date:** 2026
- **URL:** https://www.atlassian.com/software/loom/pricing
- **Retrieved:** 2026-06
- **Claim:** Free Starter plan with basic recording; paid Business/AI plans in the mid-teens per user/mo, with higher enterprise options.
- **Used in deck:** slides 3, 4, 8

## Tella — Pro ~$6.50/mo and Premium ~$9.50/mo on annual billing

- **Source:** Tella pricing pages
- **Date:** 2026
- **URLs:** https://www.tella.com/pricing · https://www.tella.com/premium
- **Retrieved:** 2026-06
- **Claim:** Pro ≈ $78/year (~$6.50/mo) or $13 monthly; Premium ≈ $114/year (~$9.50/mo) or $19 monthly; Enterprise custom. Creator-grade editing — own voiceover, multiple takes, layouts, timeline.
- **Used in deck:** slides 3, 4, 5, 8 (the $9 anchor sits just above Tella's annual band)

## Screen Studio — one-time license ~$89 (extended/commercial license higher)

- **Source:** Screen Studio site; TrustRadius
- **Date:** 2026
- **URLs:** https://screen.studio · https://www.trustradius.com/products/screen-studio/pricing
- **Retrieved:** 2026-06
- **Claim:** macOS app sold as a one-time license starting ~$89, with a more expensive extended license; sometimes treated as ~$108/year or ~$29/mo equivalent in reviews. Polished, animated demos with auto zooms and tuneable pacing.
- **Used in deck:** slides 3, 4, 5
