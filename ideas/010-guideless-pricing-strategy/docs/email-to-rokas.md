You asked what price point I'd be comfortable with — and rather than just give you my number, I went and looked at where Guideless actually sits next to Loom, Tella, Screen Studio, and Guidde. It turned into a short deck, which I've attached.

The short version: the magic of Guideless is the zero-editing, AI-narrated workflow, but it's intentionally lighter on editing than the tools I'd pay top dollar for. Right now your only paid step is $29/mo, sitting beside tools that give me a lot more control — so for an add-on I reach for between heavier jobs, it's a hard jump straight from free.

My actual suggestion isn't a discount for me — it's a third tier. Keep Free, keep the $29 plan as your **Team** tier, and slot a **Solo / Creator tier at around $9/mo** in between: the core magic with sensible volume limits, team features reserved for the $29 plan. That opens up the pile of solo creators and consultants who already pay for Loom or Tella and would happily add Guideless as a quick-hit utility — without touching what you charge teams.

The deck lays out the comparison and the tiers if it's useful for your product folks. Either way, genuinely love what you've built.
